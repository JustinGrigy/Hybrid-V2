Full-Body Stretches & Mobility

Incorporate these movements post-workout or on active recovery days to maintain tissue length and joint mobility.

1. World's Greatest Stretch

5 reps per side. Opens hips, thoracic spine, and hamstrings dynamically.

2. Standing Quad & Hip Flexor Stretch

45 seconds per side. Essential for runners and post-squat recovery.

3. Pigeon Pose / Figure Four Stretch

60 seconds per side. Relieves glute and piriformis tightness.

4. Doorway Chest & Bicep Stretch

45 seconds holding each arm. Counters heavy bench press and push volume.

5. Lat & Shoulder Child's Pose

60 seconds. Reaches overhead to decompress lats and upper back.

6. Hamstring Wall Stretch

60 seconds per side. Great for lower back decompression post-deadlift.
